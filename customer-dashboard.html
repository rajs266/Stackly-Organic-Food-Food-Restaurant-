Frequent Questions

Quick answers to common questions about farm orders
How do you guarantee perishable freshness during transit?
All deliveries travel in our specialized fleet of temperature-controlled refrigerated vans kept at a steady 3.8°C to 4.2°C, packaged with biodegradable wool insulation liners and food-grade freeze packs.
What is your return & refund guarantee?
We offer a 100% Farm Fresh Guarantee. If any fruit or vegetable arrives damaged or sub-par, notify us within 48 hours for an instant full replacement or account credit.
Can I skip a week or pause my subscription?
Yes! You can pause, skip, or modify your weekly delivery anytime before Tuesday 11:59 PM directly from your Orders or Account Settings tab without any cancellation fees.
How do I redeem my loyalty reward points?
Go to the Loyalty & Rewards section above to copy your unlocked coupon codes or click "Redeem" on specific farm perks like avocado boxes and raw honey jars.
